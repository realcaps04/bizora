The product

This is the desktop app.

The same sidebar, tables and actions you get after install. Switch the screen — nothing here is a laptop mockup.

Features

Everything for the day’s billing.

GST invoices

Cash sales and full B2B bills, with HSN, CGST, SGST or IGST. Pick a saved GST business by name. Download any invoice as PDF or Excel.

Quotations

Send a validity date with the items, then convert the same quotation into an invoice.

UPI QR on the bill

Save a UPI ID once. The printed invoice carries a QR with the amount payable already in it.

Products and stock

Purchase rate, selling rate, GST and quantity on every item. Record sales returns and purchase returns so stock stays correct.

Customers

Phone, GSTIN, place, purchase history and outstanding balances in one directory.

Reports

Sales, purchases, GST, stock, collections and a profit overview for the dates you pick.

Invoicing

Create a GST invoice in one screen.

Pick the customer, add catalogue items, set qty and rate. Tax splits itself. Edit an existing invoice without changing the number.

  • HSN, GST, discounts and place of supply
  • Paid, partial and unpaid, with UPI, cash or bank
  • Print or save as PDF from the same document

Inventory

Know what is on the shelf.

Search the catalogue, keep purchase and selling prices, and watch stock move when you buy or sell.

  • Opening stock, low-stock flags, HSN and GST
  • Purchases raise stock; sales bring it down
  • Import a category catalogue, then sell from it

People and paper

Customers, purchases, staff.

Keep GSTIN and outstanding on the customer. Record a supplier bill. Give cashiers a smaller set of actions than the owner.

  • Customer balances next to invoice history
  • Purchase entry with rate, GST and amount
  • Owner, manager, cashier and staff roles

Desktop application

Your books stay on this machine.

Bizora is installed on Windows. Day-to-day invoices, stock and customers are not kept in a browser tab. Optional Google Drive backup is there if you want a copy off the PC.

Works offline

Create invoices and look up stock with no internet connection.

Local backups

A password-protected backup file on this computer, restore when you need it.

In-app updates

When this PC is online, Bizora can check GitHub for a newer installer.
