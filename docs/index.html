Everything a counter needs

Bizora is a desktop program. Invoices, stock, and customer records stay in the workspace on that PC.

GST invoices

Tax invoices with HSN, GST, place of supply, and your bank details on the PDF.

Quotations

Send a quotation with the same layout, validity date, and a UPI QR for the quoted total.

Amount-based UPI QR

Enter a UPI ID once. Each bill generates a QR for the balance due or the quotation total.

Products for your trade

Start from a catalog for your business type, then keep prices and stock on your company only.

Customers and purchases

Save customers, record purchases and expenses, and take payments against invoices.

On this PC

Day-to-day bills run locally. Lock the screen with a PIN when you step away from the counter.

Install Bizora on Windows

The setup file is hosted on GitHub. Run it, choose a folder, and open Bizora from the Start menu. Your business files stay on the computer after uninstall unless you remove them yourself.
